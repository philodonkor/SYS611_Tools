The penalty kick

A soccer game is tied, and a penalty kick will decide the winner. The referee checks that both the kicker and goalkeeper are ready, then signals the kick to proceed.

Model everything from that signal through the recorded outcome, using only the control structures the behavior actually requires. Model control flow, not physical dynamics.

Your model must account for

  • An attempt that misses the target entirely
  • An on-target attempt the goalkeeper stops
  • An on-target attempt that results in a goal

Leave out of the model

  • Events before the referee's signal or after the outcome is recorded
  • Continuous physical behavior, such as the ball's trajectory
